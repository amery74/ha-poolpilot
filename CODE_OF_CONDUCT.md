# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free environment for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on the project and the code.
- Respect different levels of experience.
- Do not publish private information or security-sensitive details.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption and publication of private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that do not respect these rules and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report serious conduct issues privately to the maintainer through GitHub.
